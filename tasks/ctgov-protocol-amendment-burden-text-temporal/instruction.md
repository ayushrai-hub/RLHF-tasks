Build an offline R model that predicts protocol amendment burden from the ClinicalTrials.gov-style records in /app/data/train.csv and /app/data/validation.csv, then score every record in /app/data/test.csv. The 2025 test target is intentionally unavailable. Use the validation period honestly, refit on all labeled records for the final predictions, and pay attention to discrimination, calibration, temporal robustness, final positive-rate plausibility, and performance for the supplied sponsor and disease subgroups. The container includes R with stats, jsonlite, and rpart, plus optional helpers under /app/helpers.

Create /app/output/predictions.csv, /app/output/validation_predictions.csv, and /app/output/metrics.json. Both CSV files must use exactly row_id,pred_label,pred_proba_positive; the first covers all 410 2025 test rows and the second covers all 260 validation rows, each sorted by row_id. metrics.json must contain exactly n_train, n_test, primary_metric_value, validation_balanced_accuracy, validation_rare_disease_balanced_accuracy, validation_brier, predicted_positive_rate, model_family, missing_value_strategy, text_feature_strategy, final_fit_rows, and temporal_split_year.

The submission is graded on two evaluations. On the visible validation set the model must reach an AUROC of at least 0.80, a balanced accuracy of at least 0.69, a Brier score no higher than 0.14, and a rare-disease balanced accuracy of at least 0.60. On the held-out 2025 test set it must reach an AUROC of at least 0.77, a balanced accuracy of at least 0.69, and a Brier score no higher than 0.16; positive recall for the small-biotech sponsor subgroup must be at least 0.60, rare-disease balanced accuracy at least 0.61, the final predicted positive rate must fall between 0.24 and 0.36, and the held-out Brier score must beat a predict-the-training-prevalence baseline by at least 0.01.

The complete metric definitions, row-count rules, and exact acceptance targets are in /app/task_contract.md. Read that contract before implementing, but choose the feature representation and modeling approach yourself. In particular, do not optimize only for validation AUROC: the hidden 2025 check also enforces balanced accuracy, calibration, small-biotech recall, rare-disease balanced accuracy, and a plausible 2025 positive prediction rate. The test-set probabilities are also checked for genuine spread (at least 40 distinct values after rounding to four decimals and a standard deviation of at least 0.07), and predictions that reproduce the held-out labels too exactly are rejected, so degenerate or near-perfect outputs will not pass.
