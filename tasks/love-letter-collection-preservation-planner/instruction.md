Build the love letter collection preservation planner under /app for handwritten letters, photographs, and keepsakes spanning decades. The heirloom-preservation Bash toolchain scans letterfolio artifact descriptors from /app/heirloom-archive/letterfolio with collection policy at /app/heirloom-archive/collection.json, materializes a certified collection capture at /app/state/collection-snapshot.json, era redundancy bind at /app/state/redundancy-pools.json, format migration rollup at /app/state/migration-rollup.json, cross-reference index ledger at /app/state/index-ledger.json, preservation schedule staging at /app/state/preservation-staging.json, intake registry at /app/state/ingest-manifest.json, then emits a preservation atlas to /app/output/preservation-atlas.json and a preservation summary to /app/output/preservation-report.json.

Implement heirloom-collection-intake and heirloom-preservation-publish per /app/docs/cli.md, /app/docs/letterfolio-format.md, /app/docs/collection-snapshot.md, /app/docs/redundancy-pools.md, /app/docs/migration-rollup.md, /app/docs/index-ledger.md, /app/docs/preservation-staging.md, /app/docs/ingest-manifest.md, /app/docs/priority-seeding.md, /app/docs/preservation-wave-bands.md, /app/docs/storage-budget.md, /app/docs/preservation-atlas-schema.md, /app/docs/preservation-report-schema.md, and /app/docs/publish-stage.md. The decoy helper sentimental_merge.sh under /app/heirloom-preservation/lib/decoy is for sentimental sorting experiments and is not on the publish hot path.

Intake scans every .letterfolio descriptor plus collection.json from an archive root with default /app/heirloom-archive, computes priority-weighted migration queue per priority-seeding.md, builds round-one migration pairs with fragile-pair avoidance, groups migrations into preservation wave bands per preservation-wave-bands.md, enforces per-artifact redundancy caps and collection storage_byte_budget per storage-budget.md, and writes all bind artifacts under /app/state with collection_snapshot_hash and manifest_hash witness bindings per the bind contracts. Publish must read only certified bind artifacts, verify manifest alignment including within_storage_budget, refuse runs when bind files are missing or witness digests disagree, and write /app/output/preservation-atlas.json plus /app/output/preservation-report.json without re-scanning letterfolio fragments.

When HEIRLOOM_ARCHIVE_ROOT is set to an absolute directory, intake must read letterfolio inputs only from that path instead of the default. Re-running intake on unchanged letterfolio input must leave collection_snapshot_hash stable while advancing run_sequence on ingest-manifest per ingest-manifest.md. Re-running publish on unchanged certified bind must yield byte-identical preservation-atlas.json and a stable report_fingerprint.
