Milestone 2 adds deferred backend policy and scope isolation on top of the milestone 1 ledger pipeline. Read /app/docs/gateway-session-integrity.md first, then /app/docs/deferred-reload.md, /app/docs/enforcement-ledger.md, /app/docs/admission-bind.md, /app/docs/admission-snapshot.md, /app/docs/state-digest.md, /app/docs/session-checkpoint.md, /app/docs/checkpoint-chain.md, /app/docs/refill-anchor.md, and /app/docs/run-pipeline.md.

queue_reload stages policy without applying it; replay_pending applies queued policies in enqueue order only when reload_scope matches scope_gen. Stale queue entries must never change live buckets but replay_pending still clears the queue after running. Clear the pending queue after every replay_pending. fresh_start bumps scope_gen, aligns reload_scope, clears queued policy and buckets, and resets refill accrual across scope boundaries.

state_digest and checkpoint binding must follow /app/docs/enforcement-ledger.md and /app/docs/state-digest.md using ledger-sealed fields. When reload_scope trails scope_gen, digest pending_reload_count is zero even if the live queue still has entries; output pending_count always reports live queue length. Output keeps milestone 1 fields plus state_digest.
