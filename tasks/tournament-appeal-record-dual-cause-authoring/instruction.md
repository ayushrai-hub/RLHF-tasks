The local Rookline tournament recorder under `/app/environment` must be brought into agreement with the rule documents at `/app/environment/docs/rulebook.md` and `/app/environment/docs/proof_contract.md`. Use the project workflow `/app/environment/scripts/run_public_cases.sh` to rebuild the recorder and regenerate `/app/output/tournament-appeal-proof.json`. The final proof must use schema `rookline.tournament-appeal-proof.v1`, `generated_by` value `rookline prove`, include `source_fingerprint` and `case_count`, and give each case a `judge` object with `verdict`, `errors`, and `accepted_match_count`, plus match records, appeal records, move traces and a standings table. Each match record reports `epoch_used` as the eligibility epoch applied when that record is scored, appeal replays use the appeal's `replay_epoch`.

A valid standings table counts only signed match authority that is eligible at the epoch being scored. When an appeal vacates a match and supplies a replay, the `replay_epoch` controls eligibility and revocation status. A revoked player at that epoch produces `revoked_at_replay_epoch:<player-id>` and the vacated match contributes no win, loss, draw or points. Move annotations are legal only when their canonical label is exactly `clean`, `tempo`, `appeal` or `legacy`; labels such as `appeal-waiver` are rejected as `illegal_annotation:<label>` rather than aliases. Rejection reasons appear in match `errors` and move-trace `reason` fields using that `<code>:<detail>` form. Standings sort by points descending, then wins descending, then player id ascending. Legacy v1 signed records before revocation remain accepted when their signatures and moves are legal. Static edits to `/app/output/tournament-appeal-proof.json` are not enough; rerunning the workflow must regenerate the same contract from the local records.
