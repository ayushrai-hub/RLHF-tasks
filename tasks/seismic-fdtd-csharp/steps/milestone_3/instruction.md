Build the interpretation layer on top of the wave engine: reverse-time migration of recorded reflections, amplitude-versus-offset characterisation, a quality-metric report, and an acquisition-level source-spacing study.

Add four subcommands to /app/seismic: `image <input.json> <output_dir>` for RTM, `avo <input.json> <output.csv>` for the Shuey two-term trend, `qc <input.json> <output.json>` for the metric report, and `sweep <input.json> <output_dir>` for the source-spacing parametric study. The full schemas (configuration fields and output formats) live at /app/docs/imaging_schema.md.

Reverse-time migration forward-propagates the source through the same FDTD engine (PML enabled), saves the forward `vz` wavefield at every step, back-propagates the recorded shot gather by injecting receiver samples in reverse time order at the receiver locations, and forms the zero-lag cross-correlation imaging condition `I(x, z) = sum_t U_forward(x, z, t) * U_backward(x, z, t)`, written as `image.npy` of shape `(nz, nx)`. AVO picks the peak absolute `vz` per receiver in the configured time window, converts offset to incidence angle by the straight-ray rule `tan(theta) = (|xr - xs| / 2) / (reflector_depth - source_z)`, fits `amplitude = intercept + gradient * sin^2(theta)`, and emits the CSV with a trailing `fit,intercept,gradient` row. QC reports `snr_db = 20*log10(peak / rms_noise)`, dominant wavelength `overburden_vp / dominant_frequency`, the quarter-wavelength vertical resolution, and per-target illumination counts under the midpoint-binning rule from the schema. Sweep evaluates the same illumination rule across the requested source spacings and writes `parameters.csv` and `illumination_map.npy` of shape `(n_spacings, n_target_xs)`.

The imaging, AVO, and QC configs read the model grids and simulation outputs they operate on from the paths named in their `model_dir`, `shot_gather`, `time_axis`, and `source.path` fields, all under /app/output. Those upstream artefacts come from the milestone 1 and 2 subcommands, so produce the referenced model grids, source wavelets, and simulations before invoking the interpretation commands, then write each command's result to the output path passed on the command line. The fixture JSONs under /app/fixtures hold the full per-command configuration.
