We're rolling out post-quantum hybrid key exchange across internal services. The witness at /app/bin/keyshare_witness merges the three handshake shards into one stream in global time order, walks each observation through the gate ladder (type, required, service, banned or rescued, quota, rate, phase) and writes /app/output/expected.json.

The report holds four top level keys: decisions, by_service (only the registered services), by_verdict (full closed enum), and summary (totals plus report_digest). Each shard line carries observation_id, service_id, observed_ts_ns, offered_groups, client_id. Service inventory, group catalog plus aliases, rollout schedule, rate caps, monthly quota anchors and caps, HMAC pin seals plus key, and client tier overrides all sit under /app/data/. The sort chain, gate order, digest templates, and the verdict closed enum HYBRID_PQ_OK, CLASSIC_OK, DEPRECATED_GRACE, PRE_ROLLOUT_PASS, SEAL_RESCUED, POLICY_DOWNGRADE_BLOCKED, GROUP_BANNED, RATE_LIMITED, QUOTA_EXHAUSTED, REJECTED_TYPE, UNKNOWN_SERVICE, INVALID live in /app/data/admission_policy/output_envelope.json.

Wrinkles: a valid HMAC pin rescues a GROUP_BANNED row to SEAL_RESCUED and bypasses quota, rate, and phase; a wrong or missing pin keeps the banned verdict. Aliases resolve to canonical names before classification and matched_group surfaces the canonical. Per-client overrides replace policy_tier. The quota counter only ticks at the quota gate. pq_mandatory blocks classic or deprecated only handshakes once the rollout window finishes, grace included. matched_group is empty otherwise.
