Fix the Rust znctl utility under `/app/environment`. It ingests DNS-like master files with `$ORIGIN` and `$INCLUDE`, merges scope seeds, and writes record catalogs, equivalence reports, and zone text under `<workroot>/.state/`.

Build with `CARGO_TARGET_DIR=/tmp/znctl-build cargo build --manifest-path /app/environment/Cargo.toml`; the verifier invokes `/tmp/znctl-build/debug/znctl`.

After init and apply-scope, a lone cold ingest often looks plausible. Failures appear in longer workflows: nested include reloads attach holders from the wrong anchor scope, lane order disagrees with include visit order, carry totals diverge across reload passes, deleting persisted reconciliation state mid-workflow fails to converge, and equivalence observations drift from catalog bodies while emitted zone text still looks superficially consistent. Re-running apply-scope or reload without further master edits must reproduce the prior pass when no master text changed.

The verifier exercises init, apply-scope, normalize, and reload against fixture cases `m1`–`m3` and scope seeds `s1`–`s3`. Commands regenerate `<workroot>/.state/record-catalog.jsonl` (owner, rtype, class, ttl, rdata, key), `<workroot>/.state/equiv-report.jsonl` (owner, body_digest, zone_digest), and `<workroot>/.state/emitted.zone` with zone lines in lane order matching the catalog. Binary layouts, reload reconciliation, and digest rules are in `/app/environment/docs/toolchain.md` and `/app/environment/docs/reload-path.md`. Generated products under `<workroot>/.state/` must come from those commands — do not hand-edit them.
