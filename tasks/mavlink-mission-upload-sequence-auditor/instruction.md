The mission upload pipeline under `/app` normalizes field `.mseq` logs into SQLite at `/app/data/missions.db` and emits structured mission summaries through `/app/bin/mission-export`. After a firmware change, upload QC reports disagree with ingested waypoint geometry and distance totals on otherwise valid logs.

Normative contracts: `/app/docs/mseq-format.md`, `/app/docs/mission-ingest-rules.md`, `/app/docs/mission-rollup-rules.md`, `/app/docs/export-schema.md`, `/app/docs/vehicle-profile.md`, `/app/docs/db-schema.md`, and `/app/docs/cli.md`. Export `exported_at_unix` must follow the upload-scoped max-`seq` rule in `/app/docs/db-schema.md` (not global max `seq` or last-inserted row order). Implement fixes in Rust under `/app/src/` and recompile with `/app/scripts/oracle-build.sh` (see `/app/docs/cli.md` Build); `/app/bin/mission-ingest` and `/app/bin/mission-export` are the compiled CLIs — use `mission-ingest --log` and `mission-export --out` per the CLI reference, not wrapper scripts.
