Now wire up range scans and deletion on top of the index from the previous milestone, still building with `cd /app && make` to `/app/bin/bpt` and still standard-library only. Everything new is specified in `/app/docs/bptree-spec.md` (sections 6 and 7); the node format, split rules and serialization from before are unchanged and get reused as-is.

`bpt range <idx> <lo> <hi>` walks the leaf chain and prints every entry whose key is in the inclusive range, one `key<tab>value` per line in ascending order. `bpt apply <idx-in> <ops-file> <idx-out>` loads an existing index, applies a mix of inserts and deletes in order, and writes the rebalanced result to `<idx-out>` without disturbing `<idx-in>`; the output must again be byte-exact. Deletion is the hard part: underflowing nodes have to be repaired by borrowing from or merging with a sibling, with the sibling preference, the separator-key rotation and the merge-vs-borrow choice all exactly as the spec lays out, and the effects propagated up to a possible root collapse. There are many delete and mixed scenarios under `/app/data` that push underflow cascades, borrow-then-merge boundaries and root collapse; the resulting trees must still satisfy every B+tree invariant and serialize to the expected bytes.
