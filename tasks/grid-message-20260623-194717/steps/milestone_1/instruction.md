This is the first milestone of a three milestone project. The purpose is to build an FSK demodulator that locks to an incoming carrier and returns a decoded ascii message.

The purpose of this first milestone is to read a frame of ADC samples and calculate the frequency using a control loop of any type. The test harness will test that you can track the incoming samples of a sine wave of frequency between 58-62Hz to within 0.005Hz. The test harness for this milestone implements a VCO and a 12 bit ADC with a 2048 sample DMA buffer. The ADC is sampling the sine wave at 2048 times the frequency which you specify as you track the incoming sine wave. The ADC is buffered by a mock DMA to provide a buffer of 2048 samples of the sine wave. This should be close to one cycle of the sine wave. In this milestone you will receive a buffer of 2048 samples and you will provide an update to the VCO frequency. You are provided with a scaffold file named /app/grid_pll.c and /app/headers/grid_pll.h. This file will be compiled with gcc and will not be a Zephyr build.

The frame size is 2048 and the ADC samples one frame when fill_adc_buf is called. The samples are unsigned uint16_t with an offset of 2048. The text fixture will call grid_pll_update repeatedly and change the sampling frequency based on the return value. You will be tested to verify that the frequency that you track settles to within 0.005Hz in less than 350 Frames and remain within that tolerance for 10 consecutive frames.
