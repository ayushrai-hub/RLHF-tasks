Mineral exploration teams operate GeoSynth AI from /app/bin/geosynth to fuse seismic traces, gravity anomalies, magnetic field readings, borehole logs, geochemical assays, and hyperspectral tiles into ranked subsurface formation hypotheses with explainable evidence chains. The engine produces a Subsurface Discovery Report that explains why an exploration block is promising instead of emitting a single classification score.

Authoritative contracts live in /app/docs/survey-ingest-contract.md, /app/docs/depth-epoch-contract.md, /app/docs/voxel-fusion-contract.md, /app/docs/hypothesis-guard-contract.md, /app/docs/discovery-report-contract.md, /app/docs/confidence-witness-math.md, and /app/docs/verifier-overlay-contract.md. Survey lanes use /app/data/surveys/seismic/traces.jsonl, /app/data/surveys/gravity/anomalies.jsonl, /app/data/surveys/magnetic/field.jsonl, /app/data/surveys/borehole/logs.jsonl, /app/data/surveys/geochem/samples.jsonl, and /app/data/surveys/hyperspectral/tiles.jsonl per survey-ingest-contract.md. Modality weights use /app/data/policies/modality-weights.json for voxel blending coefficients. The policy file /app/data/policies/formation-governance.json supplies block_guard_pairs, confidence_floor, and hypothesis_priority. Rebuild the Rust engine with /app/scripts/rebuild-geosynth-engine.sh after patching pipeline sources under /app.

load-surveys writes /app/state/survey-ingest-catalog.json and /app/state/survey-seq-ledger.json per the binding summary in survey-ingest-contract.md. depth-epoch writes /app/state/depth-epoch-ledger.json as defined in /app/docs/depth-epoch-contract.md. fuse-voxels writes /app/state/voxel-staging-snapshot.json and /app/state/voxel-fusion-graph.json with forward-pair edges per the binding summary in voxel-fusion-contract.md. guard-hypotheses writes /app/state/hypothesis-guard-ledger.json and must honor block_guard_pairs as defined in /app/docs/hypothesis-guard-contract.md.

score-confidence writes /app/state/confidence-margin-ledger.json with conf margin lines capped by confidence_floor documented in confidence-witness-math.md. branch-formations writes /app/state/formation-compose-staging.json where compose array order follows hypothesis_priority per discovery-report-contract.md. Bundled copper-belt evidence maps sample_id tr-gc-001 to pathfinder-spike evidence_kind. export-discovery writes /app/output/geosynth-discovery-report.json per discovery-report-contract.md. discovery-seal writes /app/state/discovery-export-bind.json when digests align per confidence-witness-math.md. /app/scripts/finalize-discovery-atlas.sh writes /app/state/discovery-atlas.ok when bind digests match live artifacts.

Quarantine decoy at /app/data/decoy/decoy-static-formation.json must not affect load-surveys.
