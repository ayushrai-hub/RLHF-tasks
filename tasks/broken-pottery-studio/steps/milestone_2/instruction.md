Two pricing adjustments in /app are not being applied correctly.

Students who have reached the qualifying senior age receive a 10% discount on their session booking. The discount is applied after any group discount that has already been applied, so the 10% is calculated on the post-group-discount amount. The system is applying this rate to a slightly wrong group of students.

Weekend evening sessions carry a peak-demand surcharge of 1.2x. The surcharge must only apply when a booking falls on both a weekend day and an evening time slot — it should not activate when just one of those conditions is met.
