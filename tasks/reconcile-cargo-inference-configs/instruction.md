The inference crate for our sentiment-transformer text classifier is being cut for release, and three sources describe its configuration in mutually contradictory ways: the platform team's release dossier, the MLflow export at /app/sources/mlflow-export.yaml, and the crate's own /app/crate/Cargo.toml and /app/crate/serving.toml. The dossier is the authoritative narrative — it defines, in prose, which source wins for each field and the exceptions that override those defaults. Several of its rules were revised after production incidents, so earlier statements in the document are superseded by later ones.

Build a TypeScript pipeline that reads the full dossier on standard input and writes three artifacts that must stay mutually consistent: the reconciled /app/release-plan.yaml, a deployment descriptor /app/deploy.json derived from it, and a /app/lock.json that binds the two by digest. Resolve model identity and evaluation provenance, quantization mode, the crate feature set, evaluation thresholds (derived from the metrics history under /app/sources), and serving limits according to the dossier; derive the deployment descriptor and lockfile per the dossier's appendices; and follow every output contract exactly. Make it runnable as `node /app/dist/reconcile.js` with the dossier piped on standard input; the dossier text itself is at /app/dossier.md.

Exact schemas and derivation formulas for all three files are in the dossier's Appendix O (plan) and Appendix D (deploy.json, lock.json). Note: min_accuracy is the selected metrics row's accuracy to three decimals with no adjustment; max_latency_ms is that row's latency times 1.2, rounded up; queue_capacity is max_batch_size times max_concurrency. The contract is not the hard part — recovering the correct input values is, and the dossier settles those through dated decisions where the latest date wins.
